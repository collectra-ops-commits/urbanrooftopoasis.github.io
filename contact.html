Quick Answers

Questions before
you submit?

Is the 2D Mood Board really free?+
Yes — completely free with zero obligation. You submit your rooftop video and a short form, and we deliver a personalised 2D Mood Board with a plant palette matched to your goals and an itemised quote within 48 hours. No payment of any kind until you decide to proceed.
What kind of video should I send?+
Just walk slowly around your rooftop holding your phone. 60–90 seconds is ideal. Show the floor surface, all four edges, any existing structures or raised areas, and the view from the rooftop. Good lighting makes it much easier for us to design accurately. Portrait or landscape is fine.
Do I need to know my exact roof size?+
Not at all. A rough estimate is completely fine — "about 80 square metres" or "roughly 20×15 feet" works perfectly. We verify the exact dimensions during the site assessment, which happens after you confirm the design. The quote we send is based on your estimate and adjusted after we visit.
What happens after I submit?+
You'll receive a confirmation on WhatsApp and email immediately. Within 48 hours we'll send your personalised 2D Mood Board — a PDF with your plant palette, an overhead layout sketch of your rooftop, and a fully itemised quote. If you love it, we can proceed. If you'd like a full 3D render first, that requires a small refundable design deposit.
My building is not in Gulshan, Banani or Bashundhara. Can you still help?+
We're currently focusing on Gulshan, Banani and Bashundhara to ensure the highest quality of service during our launch phase. If you're in another Dhaka area, WhatsApp us directly — we're expanding soon and may be able to accommodate you depending on location. We'll be honest about whether we can serve you well right now.
Can I just call or WhatsApp instead of filling the form?+
Absolutely — WhatsApp us at +880 1859-929858 anytime. However the form is the fastest way to get your free design, because it gives us everything we need to start immediately. A WhatsApp conversation will eventually need the same information, so the form saves time on both sides.
