Every Visit

What our experts
do on every visit.

Every maintenance visit follows a strict protocol. Nothing is skipped. Nothing is guessed. Every action is logged and reported back to you.

🧪
Soil pH Testing
We test soil pH at every visit using a calibrated meter. Ideal range for rooftop plants is 6.0–7.0. Results recorded and compared to your Month 0 baseline.
🌱
Nutrient & Fertiliser Top-Up
Bundle-specific fertiliser formulation applied based on the plant type and season. Slow-release and organic options used to avoid burn.
✂️
Pruning & Shaping
Dead growth removed. Structural plants shaped. Trailing plants guided. Canopy managed for light penetration and airflow.
🐛
Pest & Disease Inspection
Full inspection of all plants for pests, fungal issues, and disease. Organic treatment applied on the spot. Severe cases escalated with a plan.
💧
Irrigation System Check
Drip lines, timers, and emitters checked for blockages and leaks. Adjusted seasonally for Dhaka's wet and dry cycles.
🔄
Seasonal Plant Rotation
Failing or seasonal plants replaced. New seasonal accents introduced. Urban Orchard clients get fresh vegetable seedlings at appropriate visit intervals.

Questions

Frequently asked
questions.

Is the maintenance subscription mandatory? +
Every installation automatically includes Year 1 subscription — this is how we guarantee the health of your garden. Without expert maintenance, rooftop plants in Dhaka's climate will deteriorate. From Year 2 onwards, renewal is your choice. If you opt out, your installation price may be adjusted to reflect the lost recurring value.
What if a plant dies between visits? +
Send us a photo on WhatsApp immediately. Our expert will diagnose whether it's salvageable or needs replacing. Plant replacement cost (nursery cost + transport) is charged at cost — labour for replacement is included in Mid and Premium plans. In practice, our plants very rarely die because the maintenance schedule is designed to prevent it.
Can I water the plants myself between visits? +
Yes — and for bundles without drip irrigation, we'll give you a simple watering guide during your installation walkthrough. Most Aesthetic Lounge and Climate Cooler plants only need watering every 3–5 days. Urban Orchard plants need more frequent watering, which is why we strongly recommend adding the drip irrigation system.
What time of day do experts visit? +
We schedule morning visits (8AM–12PM) to avoid Dhaka's peak afternoon heat. You don't need to be home — we just need rooftop access confirmed in advance. We'll WhatsApp you 48 hours before and again on the morning of the visit.
How do I renew my subscription? +
We send you a renewal reminder 30 days before your annual date — by email and WhatsApp. You'll receive a Year 2 proposal with an updated care plan. Renewal takes 2 minutes: confirm via WhatsApp and pay the invoice. Your 3 visits are then re-scheduled automatically.
Do you service all areas of Dhaka? +
Yes — we currently serve all major Dhaka areas including Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Bashundhara, Uttara, Mirpur, Mohammadpur, Baridhara, and Motijheel. If you're outside these areas, contact us and we'll let you know if we can accommodate.
