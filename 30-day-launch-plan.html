Deploy Your Website
Free on GitHub Pages

Zero cost. Custom domain optional. Live in under 30 minutes. Do this on Day 1 before anything else.

1
Create a GitHub account
Go to github.com → Sign up free. Use your business email (hello@urbanrooftopoasis.com once set up).
2
Create a new repository
Click "New repository" → Name it exactly: urbanrooftopoasis.github.io → Set to Public → Click "Create repository"
3
Upload your index.html file
Click "uploading an existing file" → drag and drop the index.html website file provided → Commit changes.
# OR via terminal if you prefer:
git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/urbanrooftopoasis.github.io
cd urbanrooftopoasis.github.io
cp /path/to/index.html .
git add . && git commit -m "Launch URO website"
git push origin main
4
Enable GitHub Pages
In your repository → Settings → Pages → Source: Deploy from branch → Branch: main → /root → Save. Wait 2–3 minutes.
5
Your website is live! 🎉
URL: https://urbanrooftopoasis.github.io — Share this immediately. Works perfectly on mobile. Free forever.
6
Update before going live
In index.html, find and replace: +880 1XXX-XXXXXX with your real WhatsApp number. Update the WhatsApp href links too. Replace the Instagram/Facebook links with your real profiles.
Free custom domain (optional): If you later want www.urbanrooftopoasis.com — domains cost ~৳1,500/year from Namecheap. Point it to GitHub Pages via CNAME record. But for launch: the .github.io URL works perfectly. Don't delay launch for a domain.

আপনার বা আপনার পরিচিত কারো ছাদ থাকলে আমরা ৪৮ ঘণ্টার মধ্যে একটা free 2D design পাঠাই।

আমাদের website: [your github link]

আপনি কি কাউকে refer করতে পারবেন? 🌿"

Building Darwan / Manager — On-Ground Script
Ground
"ভাই, আমি Urban Rooftop Oasis থেকে এসেছি। আমরা ঢাকার building-এর ছাদে premium garden তৈরি করি। আপনাদের building-এর owner-কে কি একটু এই card দেওয়া যাবে? আমরা free design দিই — কোনো commitment লাগবে না। Owner যদি interested হন, আমার WhatsApp-এ contact করতে বলবেন।"

[Hand over the printed A5 card with QR code]
Key principle: Never try to hard-sell the darwan. Your goal is only to get your card to the building owner. Keep it short, respectful, and leave the card. Follow up by calling back in 3–4 days.

Real Estate Agent Partnership Pitch
Referral
"Hi [Agent Name], I run Urban Rooftop Oasis — we turn Dhaka rooftops into premium gardens. I wanted to propose a referral partnership.

Many of your property owner clients likely have unused rooftops. We transform them — and it increases property value by 10–15%. If you refer a client who installs with us, we'll pay you a referral commission of [X%] of the installation fee.

No extra work for you — just a WhatsApp introduction. Would you be open to this?"
Commission suggestion: 5–8% of installation value. On a ৳1.5 lakh install, that's ৳7,500–12,000 for one referral. Very motivating for agents.

Closing Message — Warm Lead Who Hasn't Deposited
WhatsApp
"Hi [Name], just a quick note — we're taking our first 3 founding installations in Dhaka at a special rate this month. Since you've already seen your design, I wanted to give you first access before we open it up.

Your rooftop could be transformed within [X] weeks from now. The deposit to confirm is just [amount] — fully credited to your total.

Want me to send the payment link? 🌿"
Why this works: Creates genuine urgency (3 slots), acknowledges the existing relationship (they've seen the design), makes the deposit feel small and safe (credited to total). Never pressure — one message, then wait 3 days before one final follow-up.
