কোল্ড চেইন প্রযুক্তি — Technology

Why our cold chain is
different from regular couriers

A regular courier van uses a compressor — like a household fridge — that needs the engine running to stay cold. Stop the engine, the temperature rises. Sit in Dhaka traffic for two hours, the temperature rises. That's not acceptable for frozen food.

Pathan uses a eutectic freezer van. Eutectic cold plates are pre-charged at a depot to −30°C before the run starts. Once charged, they release cold steadily for 12–18 hours — no engine, no electricity, no compressor needed on the road. Your food stays at −18°C whether the van is moving or stuck at a checkpoint.

This is the same technology used in pharmaceutical cold chains and international frozen food exports. We've brought it to the Dhaka–Sylhet route.

❄️
−18°C Maintained
Eutectic plates pre-charged to −30°C. Holds temperature for 12–18 hours — well beyond our 6-hour highway run.
⚡
No Electricity on Road
Unlike compressor vans, eutectic technology doesn't need the engine running to maintain cold. Traffic delays don't affect your food.
🔒
Sealed Cargo Hold
The cargo compartment is only opened at scheduled stops — Dhaka loading and Sylhet unloading. No mid-route temperature breaks.
📋
Batch Integrity
Every package in the van shares the same cold environment. This is why we reject partially thawed food at pickup — one warm item raises the whole hold.
নিয়মাবলী — Rules

What you need to know before booking

Simple rules that protect your food and everyone else's in the same run.

🧊
Food Must Be Rock-Solid Frozen
খাবার অবশ্যই সম্পূর্ণ জমাট হতে হবে

When our rider arrives, your food must be completely frozen — no soft spots, no partial thawing. Our rider will check firmness at the door. Partially thawed food will be refused, no exceptions. Pre-freeze at least 24 hours before pickup day.

📦
Packaging Must Be Sealed & Labelled
প্যাকেজিং সিলড ও লেবেলযুক্ত হতে হবে

All food must be in sealed, leak-proof packaging — zip-lock bags, sealed containers, or wrapped securely in cling film then placed in a bag. Label your package with sender name, recipient name, and phone number. We provide labels on request.

⏰
Book Before Thursday 10 PM
বৃহস্পতিবার রাত ১০টার আগে বুক করুন

The booking window closes Thursday 10 PM sharp. Late bookings cannot be accommodated — the manifest is locked and the rider schedule is set. Plan ahead. We send a reminder every Tuesday via WhatsApp to regular customers.

🚫
Items We Cannot Carry
যা আমরা বহন করতে পারি না

Non-food items, liquids that are not frozen solid, fresh (unfrozen) perishables, items above 20 kg per booking without prior arrangement, and any item that cannot be legally transported between districts. When in doubt, ask us first on WhatsApp.

প্রশ্ন — FAQ

Common questions

If your question isn't here, send us a WhatsApp — we reply fast.

What if the run doesn't confirm that week? রান কনফার্ম না হলে কী হবে?
▼
If bookings don't reach the 60% minimum threshold by Thursday 10 PM, the run is postponed and your deposit is fully refunded within 24 hours via the same payment method. You'll be notified by WhatsApp as soon as the decision is made — no later than Friday 8 AM.
Can I send in both directions — Sylhet to Dhaka as well? সিলেট থেকে ঢাকাতেও পাঠাতে পারব?
▼
Yes. The van runs both ways every week — Dhaka to Sylhet on Friday night, Sylhet to Dhaka on Saturday night. You can book reverse-route shipments the same way. Same rules apply for the return run.
How do I track my order after booking? বুকিংয়ের পরে অর্ডার ট্র্যাক করব কীভাবে?
▼
Once booked, you receive a booking reference number. You can use this on our Track page to check your order status — Booked, Picked Up, In Transit, or Delivered. We also send WhatsApp updates at each stage automatically.
What types of food can I send? কী ধরনের খাবার পাঠাতে পারব?
▼
Any homemade food that can be frozen — curries, rice dishes, Sylheti specialties like Satkara preparations, pithas, hilsa fish, beef and mutton dishes, and more. The food must be frozen solid before handover. We cannot carry fresh (unfrozen) food, liquids, or non-food items.
What is the minimum and maximum I can send? সর্বনিম্ন ও সর্বোচ্চ কত কেজি পাঠাতে পারব?
▼
Minimum is 1 kg per booking. Maximum is 20 kg per booking without prior arrangement. For larger shipments above 20 kg, contact us on WhatsApp first and we'll arrange accordingly.
What if my food arrives damaged or thawed? খাবার নষ্ট বা গলে গেলে কী হবে?
▼
Contact us on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery with a photo of the issue. We investigate every cold chain complaint seriously. If the fault is on our end, we will arrange compensation or a free re-run on the next available week.
